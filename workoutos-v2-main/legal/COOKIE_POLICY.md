# COOKIE POLICY

**Last Updated:** August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Workout OS uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to recognize you when you use our web application.

## 1. What Technologies We Use
Unlike traditional websites that rely heavily on third-party tracking cookies for advertising, Workout OS is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that primarily uses modern browser storage mechanisms to ensure the application functions correctly.

- **Local Storage / Session Storage:** We use HTML5 Local Storage to save your UI preferences (e.g., dark mode vs. light mode), current session states, and offline data (like caching your tasks so the app loads instantly).
- **Authentication Tokens (Supabase):** When you log in, our authentication provider (Supabase) sets secure, HTTP-only cookies and local storage tokens to keep you securely logged in across sessions and to authorize your requests to our backend database.

## 2. Why We Use Them
We use these technologies strictly for **Essential / Functional purposes**:
- To remember that you are logged in.
- To protect your data via Row Level Security by passing your Auth Token.
- To persist your UI choices (language, theme).

## 3. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking
**We do not use advertising cookies.** 
We do not use Meta Pixels, TikTok Pixels, or cross-site tracking cookies. 
If we enable aggregated analytics (e.g., Vercel Analytics) to monitor application performance and crashes, these are configured in a privacy-preserving manner without cross-site tracking.

## 4. Your Controls
Because the local storage and auth cookies we use are strictly necessary for the application to function, they cannot be individually disabled without breaking the app. 
If you do not wish for us to use these technologies, you must stop using the Service and delete your account. You can also manually clear your browser's local storage and cookies at any time, which will simply log you out of Workout OS.
