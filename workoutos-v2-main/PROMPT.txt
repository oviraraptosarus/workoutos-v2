# EXAM MASTER BLUEPRINT PROMPT

I have uploaded a ZIP file containing:

* Lecture modules/PPTs
* Previous year papers
* Sessional papers
* Mid papers
* End-sem papers
* Assignments
* Lab manuals
* Sample papers
* Any other study material

Your job is **NOT** to summarize these files.

Your job is to reverse-engineer the examiner's thinking and build the **ultimate revision book ("Baby Bullet Book")** for every subject in this ZIP.

## Phase 1 — Crawl Everything

* Crawl every folder and subfolder.
* Open every PDF, PPT, DOC, DOCX, image, and text file.
* Do not skip files because they seem duplicated.
* Merge duplicates only after verifying they are identical.
* Identify all subjects automatically.
* Separate the analysis subject-wise.

---

## Phase 2 — Reverse Engineer Every Subject

For every subject:

* Determine the exact syllabus.
* Determine Units.
* Identify the programming language (if applicable).
* Ignore unrelated languages unless the concept is identical.
* Extract every question from every paper.

Create one master database.

For every question record:

* Unit
* Topic
* Subtopic
* Marks
* Question type
* Previous paper
* Year
* Concept tested

---

## Phase 3 — Find the Hidden Blueprint

Do NOT treat different wording as different questions.

Merge them into **Question Models**.

Example:

Insert into linked list

Insert node at position

Insertion in SLL

↓

Question Model:
**SLL Insertion**

Do this for every subject.

---

## Phase 4 — Frequency Analysis

For every Question Model calculate:

* Number of occurrences
* Previous papers
* Marks asked
* Variations
* Difficulty
* Units covered

Rank:

* Extremely High
* High
* Medium
* Low

using actual evidence.

---

## Phase 5 — Examiner Pattern

Identify:

* Favorite topics
* Favorite diagrams
* Favorite derivations
* Favorite proofs
* Favorite numerical problems
* Favorite algorithms
* Favorite programs
* Favorite theory questions
* Questions that always repeat
* Questions that alternate every year
* Questions that replace each other

Find hidden patterns instead of obvious ones.

---

## Phase 6 — Sure-Shot Question Bank

Create the **minimum** number of question models required to maximize marks.

Separate into:

### 2 Marks

Include every high-probability definition, difference, syntax, property, formula, short note, diagram, and concept.

---

### 5 Marks

Include every important algorithm, derivation, numerical problem, explanation, trace table, proof, program, or conceptual model.

---

### 8/10/15 Marks (depending on subject)

Include every complete question model:

* Long answers
* Derivations
* Programs
* Case studies
* Diagrams
* Numerical problems

Include every important variation.

---

## Phase 7 — Baby Bullet Book

Create a complete revision guide.

For every question include:

* Exact exam-style wording
* Marks
* Unit
* Frequency
* Confidence
* Previous appearances
* Similar variations
* Diagrams wherever needed
* Tables wherever needed
* Flowcharts wherever needed

Do NOT provide full answers unless required.

---

## Phase 8 — Killer Revision Pack

At the end of every subject produce:

### 1. 20 Most Important Questions

Exactly in university exam style.

---

### 2. 10 Must-Draw Diagrams

Exactly as expected in exams.

---

### 3. 20 One-Liners

Definitions, formulas, properties.

---

### 4. Common Examiner Traps

Mistakes students make.

---

### 5. Last Night Revision

A 5-page ultra-condensed revision containing only the highest-yield concepts.

---

### 6. 90% Marks Roadmap

If I only have:

* 6 hours
* 3 hours
* 1 hour

tell me exactly what to study in order.

---

## Phase 9 — Evidence-Based Prediction

Predict the most likely question models for the next exam using:

* Historical frequency
* Recent trends
* Syllabus coverage
* Examiner rotation

Do **not** claim certainty. Clearly state these are evidence-based probability estimates, not guarantees.

---

## Output Format

For **every subject**, generate:

* Exam Blueprint
* Frequency Analysis
* Question Models
* Sure-Shot Question Bank
* Baby Bullet Book
* 20 Most Important Questions
* Diagrams
* Last Night Revision Notes
* 90% Marks Roadmap
* Evidence-Based Prediction

Make it look like a premium exam-preparation book rather than a simple summary. Focus on maximizing expected marks with the least study time. Never invent questions or patterns that are not supported by the uploaded materials.
