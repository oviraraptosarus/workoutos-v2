# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** August 2026
**Effective Date:** August 2026

Welcome to Workout OS ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our web application (the "Service").

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) of India. For users accessing the Service from the European Union, United Kingdom, or the State of California, additional provisions are outlined in Section 10.

## 1. Age Restriction (7+)
Our Service is intended for individuals who are 7 years of age or older. 
- **Under 18 (Child Mode):** If you are under 18, we restrict the collection of sensitive data (such as progress photos) and disable AI health profiling to protect your privacy. 
- **Parental Consent:** We may require verifiable parental consent prior to account creation for users under the age of 18 (India) or 13 (US/Global), in compliance with the DPDPA and COPPA. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without required consent, we will delete that data.

## 2. The Data We Collect About You
We collect data directly from you and automatically as you interact with our Service.

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Identity & Contact Data:** Name, email address, and authentication credentials (managed via Supabase Auth).
- **Health & Biological Data:** Age, gender, weight, height, daily step counts, and caloric/macronutrient goals.
- **Fitness & Nutritional Data:** Workout logs, exercises performed, meals logged, and water intake.
- **Financial Data:** Budget tracking inputs, expenses, and income logged within the Service.
- **Biometric / Visual Data:** Progress photographs you upload to the Service.
- **Conversational Data:** Voice recordings, chat transcripts, and text prompts you submit to our AI Copilot.

### B. Information Generated or Inferred by the Service
- **Behavioral Profiles:** The Service analyzes your task completion rates, workout consistency, and chat interactions to generate "Execution Scores" and identify "Behavior Patterns" (e.g., procrastination tendencies, peak energy hours).
- **AI Memories:** If enabled, the AI Copilot extracts and permanently stores contextual facts about your life (e.g., "User dislikes morning workouts") to personalize future interactions.

### C. Automatically Collected Information
- **Telemetry & Technical Data:** IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and crash logs.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
We process your personal data for the following purposes:
- **Service Provision:** To operate the core tracking features (diet, workouts, tasks, finances).
- **AI Personalization:** To generate personalized fitness routines, task prioritization, and behavioral insights via the AI Copilot.
- **Security:** To authenticate your identity and prevent fraudulent use.
- **System Improvement:** To diagnose crashes and improve the AI models (subject to third-party limitations).

## 4. Third-Party Data Sharing & AI Processing
We do not sell your personal data to data brokers. However, to operate the Service, we share data with specific sub-processors:

- **Database & Hosting (Supabase):** All structured data and uploaded images are stored securely on Supabase servers.
- **AI Infrastructure (Google / OpenRouter):** When you interact with the AI Copilot, your prompts, voice transcripts, and uploaded food images are transmitted to Google (Gemini) or OpenRouter for processing. 
  - *Note on AI Training:* We configure our API connections to prevent third-party AI providers from using your conversational data to train their foundational models. However, data is temporarily processed by their servers to generate your immediate response.

## 5. Data Security
We implement strict security measures to protect your data:
- **Row Level Security (RLS):** Our database is configured so that your data is mathematically isolated. Only your authenticated user ID can read or write to your personal records.
- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our hosting providers.

*Disclaimer:* While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

## 6. Data Retention
We retain your personal data only for as long as your account is active. 
- If you delete your account, your profile, health logs, financial logs, progress photos, and AI memories are permanently deleted from our primary database.
- Telemetry logs and system backups may be retained for up to 90 days before automated deletion.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction:** Edit your data directly within the application settings.
- **Erasure:** Delete your account and associated data permanently via the application.
- **Withdraw Consent:** You may disable AI Memory or revoke camera/microphone permissions at any time.

## 8. Incident Response
In the event of a data breach that compromises your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant regulatory authorities in accordance with applicable laws within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.
