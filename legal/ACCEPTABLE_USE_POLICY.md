# ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

**Last Updated:** August 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") outlines the permissible and prohibited behaviors when using Workout OS (the "Service"). By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to comply with this AUP. 

Violations of this AUP may result in the immediate suspension or permanent termination of your account, deletion of your data, and reporting to law enforcement authorities where legally required.

## 1. Prohibited Content
You may not use the Service to upload, store, generate, or share content (including text, images, or AI prompts) that:
- Is sexually explicit, pornographic, or depicts non-consensual nudity. (We employ automated NSFW detection filters on image uploads).
- Constitutes Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or exploits/harms minors in any way.
- Is unlawful, fraudulent, defamatory, harassing, abusive, or threatening.
- Promotes violence, self-harm, eating disorders, or extreme caloric deficits.
- Infringes on the intellectual property, privacy, or publicity rights of any third party.

## 2. AI Abuse and Manipulation
The AI Copilot is provided to assist with execution, habit tracking, and data logging. You may not:
- Attempt to "jailbreak," prompt-inject, or bypass the safety filters and ethical guardrails of the AI Copilot.
- Use the AI Copilot to generate malware, illegal financial advice, or instructions on how to create dangerous substances.
- Interrogate the AI Copilot for medical diagnosis, psychiatric evaluation, or injury treatment protocols.
- Use automated scripts, bots, or scraping tools to extract the AI's outputs, system prompts, or underlying architecture.

## 3. Platform Security & Integrity
You agree not to engage in any activity that compromises the security or performance of the Service, including:
- Attempting to access data, accounts, or databases belonging to other users.
- Bypassing Row Level Security (RLS) policies or attempting privilege escalation.
- Uploading malicious code, viruses, or deploying Denial of Service (DoS) attacks against our infrastructure (including Supabase and Vercel endpoints).
- Reverse-engineering, decompiling, or attempting to extract the source code of the Service.

## 4. Enforcement
We reserve the right, but do not assume the obligation, to investigate any violation of this AUP. 
- We may monitor your usage, including progress photos and AI logs, if automated security flags are triggered or if required by law enforcement.
- We may remove any content or terminate any account without prior notice.
